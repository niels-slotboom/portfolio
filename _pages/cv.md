## Academic Summary
I am a theoretical physicist working at the intersection of mathematical gravitation, numerical relativity, and high-performance computing. My academic path has been driven by a deep appreciation for the geometric rigour of spacetime physics and the computational frameworks needed to simulate it. My research focuses on the entire pipeline of strong-field gravitational problems, with a particular emphasis on designing versatile parabolic/diffusion-type solvers for initial data via functional gradient descent and exploring the non-linear classical evolution regimes of higher-order curvature frameworks like Quadratic Gravity. This winter, I am beginning my PhD at the University of Nottingham to continue developing numerical methods for these extended theories of gravity. For more information, see the [about page]( {{ site.baseurl }}/about/ ) of this website
